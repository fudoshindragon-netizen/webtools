# Digital products

**Paid product files and their source are not stored in this public repository.** That includes customer deliverables (PDFs, zips, and similar) and the HTML, scripts, and build files used to produce them.

Keep those files outside the repo and upload deliverables to Gumroad, Stripe, Buy Me a Coffee, or another private store. A `.gitignore` rule ignores `digital-products/**/*.pdf`, `digital-products/**/*.zip`, and `*.zip` so packaged files are not added again by mistake.
