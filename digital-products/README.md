# Digital products

Source, listing copy, and build scripts for MicroVenture Labs products may live here.

**Paid deliverables (PDFs, zips, and other customer files) are not stored in this public repository.** Keep those files outside the repo and upload them to Gumroad, Stripe, Buy Me a Coffee, or another private store. A `.gitignore` rule ignores `digital-products/**/*.pdf`, `digital-products/**/*.zip`, and `*.zip` so they are not added again by mistake.
