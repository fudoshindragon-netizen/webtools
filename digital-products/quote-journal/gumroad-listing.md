## Description

Ninety days, one page a day. Each page carries one short quote, one plain prompt to answer in your own words, and ruled space to write. No daily reading plan to keep up with and no journaling method to learn — you read one line, you answer one question, you keep the page.

The quotes are our own, written in a dry, practical tone about plans, water, neighbors, paperwork, sleep and the small jobs that make a hard week easier. They are not attributed to anyone, because nobody said them but us. Each prompt asks for something the quote does not already say — a name, a number, a place, a date or a decision — so the page fills with your household, not our words. This is a household-preparedness journal: the prompts assume you are getting a home ready for a bad week, and they are not general-purpose journaling questions.
