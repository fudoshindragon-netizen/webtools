## Full description

**Eighteen topics. One hundred and twelve questions. A worked solution for every one. Three practice tests you can print.**

The Algebra Mastery Pack is a self-study pack for grade-11 algebra, functions and data analysis. Each topic opens with the method written out step by step, then gives you practice questions with instant feedback and a full worked solution — the steps, not just the final answer.

**What you get — instant download**

- **Algebra-Mastery-Pack.html** — the whole pack in one file. Double-click it and it runs in any browser, offline. No account, no app store, no internet needed.
- **folder-edition/** — the same pack as separate files (`index.html`, `app.js`, `styles.css`, `content.json`) for anyone who wants to serve it or read the content as plain data.
- **practice-tests/** — three printable tests (A, B and C), 24 questions each, one question from every topic, with working space and an answer line. Each test has its own answer key PDF with a worked solution for every question and a marking guide. Five pages per test, three pages per key, A4.
- **Welcome-and-Instructions.md** — how to open it, how to use it, and how to mark the tests.

**The eighteen topics**

1. Solving Linear Equations (for x)
2. Literal Equations & Solving for y
3. Linear Inequalities
4. Systems of Linear Equations
5. Functions: Notation, Domain & Range
6. Linear Functions: Slope, Intercepts & Equations
7. Quadratic Functions & Equations
8. Polynomials: Operations & Factoring
9. Radical & Rational Expressions
10. Exponential Functions & Growth/Decay
11. Data: Measures of Center & Spread
12. Scatter Plots, Line of Best Fit & Correlation
13. Absolute Value Equations & Inequalities
14. Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences
15. Inverse Functions
16. Function Transformations & Vertex Form
17. Systems of Linear Inequalities & Linear Programming
18. Probability from Two-Way Tables

**How it works**

Pick a topic from the grid, read the method and the rules or table, then answer the questions. Multiple choice: pick one, press **Check answer**. Short answer: type the number only. Your progress and a mastery percentage per topic are saved in your browser automatically — close the tab and nothing is lost. When you have worked through the topics, print Test A. Take B a week later and C a week after that; no question appears in more than one test, so the three together cover seventy-two different questions.

**Why this is the paid edition**

A free browser app covers twelve of these topics and sixty-four of these questions, and it stays free. This pack contains everything in that app **plus** six further topics (absolute value, sequences, inverse functions, transformations and vertex form, systems of inequalities and linear programming, probability from two-way tables), the remaining questions, and the three printed practice tests with answer keys that the free app does not have. If the free app is enough for you, use it — we would rather say that plainly than sell you a duplicate.

**Honest notes**

- This is a study aid, not a substitute for your course, teacher, textbook or curriculum, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any textbook publisher, OpenStax, Khan Academy or any other referenced site.
- The worked steps show one valid method, not the only one. Follow your teacher's method and the course syllabus.
- Every question was written for this pack from standard mathematical facts, and each answer was checked by substituting it back into the original problem. No publisher's text, examples or figures are reproduced.
- The external links inside each topic are free pages offered as a second explanation of the same method: Purplemath, Khan Academy, Maths Is Fun, OpenStax (free textbooks), Paul's Online Math Notes and the Desmos graphing calculator. All are free to read or use and none of them is required to use this pack.
