1. [Solving Linear Equations (for x)]

2. [Literal Equations & Solving for y]
Answer: A) w = (P − 2l) ÷ 2

Worked solution: Step 1: subtract 2l from both sides → P − 2l = 2w. Step 2: divide by 2 → w = (P − 2l) ÷ 2.

3. [Linear Inequalities]
Answer: A) x ≥ 5

Worked solution: Step 1: add 6 to both sides → 3x ≥ 15. Step 2: divide by 3 (positive, no flip) → x ≥ 5.

4. [Systems of Linear Equations]
Answer: A) (3, 2)

Worked solution: Elimination: Step 1: add the equations → 3x = 9. Step 2: x = 3. Step 3: plug back → 3 − y = 1 → y = 2. Solution: (3, 2).

5. [Functions: Notation, Domain & Range]

Worked solution: Step 1: substitute 2 for x → h(2) = 5(2) − 4. Step 2: multiply → 10 − 4. Step 3: subtract → 6.

6. [Linear Functions: Slope, Intercepts & Equations]
Answer: 3

Worked solution: Step 1: slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁) = (8 − 2) ÷ (3 − 1). Step 2: = 6 ÷ 2 = 3.

7. [Quadratic Functions & Equations]
Answer: A) downward

Worked solution: Step 1: the leading coefficient a = −1 is negative. Step 2: a negative a opens the parabola downward.

8. [Polynomials: Operations & Factoring]
Answer: 6

Worked solution: Step 1: the coefficients 6 and 12 share a factor of 6. Step 2: the GCF is 6, so 6x + 12 = 6(x + 2).

9. [Radical & Rational Expressions]
Answer: A) least common denominator (LCD)

Worked solution: Multiplying every term by the LCD clears all the fractions so you can solve the resulting equation.

10. [Exponential Functions & Growth/Decay]
Answer: 25

Worked solution: Step 1: substitute 2 for x → y = 100·(1/2)². Step 2: (1/2)² = 1/4. Step 3: 100 × (1/4) = 25.

11. [Data: Measures of Center & Spread]
Answer: A) Mean

Worked solution: The mean uses every value in the sum, so a single extreme outlier pulls it strongly. The median and mode are more resistant.

12. [Scatter Plots, Line of Best Fit & Correlation]
Answer: A) strong negative correlation

Worked solution: r near −1 means a strong linear relationship where y decreases as x increases.

13. [Absolute Value Equations & Inequalities]
Answer: 4

Worked solution: Case 1: 2x + 1 = 9 → 2x = 8 → x = 4. Case 2: 2x + 1 = −9 → 2x = −10 → x = −5. The larger solution is 4. Check: |2(4) + 1| = |9| = 9 ✓.

14. [Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences]
Answer: B) 3

Worked solution: Divide a term by the one before it: 6 ÷ 2 = 3, and 18 ÷ 6 = 3, and 54 ÷ 18 = 3. The common ratio is 3.

15. [Inverse Functions]
Answer: A) √x

Worked solution: Restricting the domain to x ≥ 0 makes squaring one-to-one, so it has an inverse: the non-negative square root, f⁻¹(x) = √x. Check: (√x)² = x for x ≥ 0 ✓.

16. [Function Transformations & Vertex Form]
Answer: A) reflects it across the x-axis

Worked solution: The minus sign outside the function negates every output: a point (x, y) becomes (x, −y). That is a reflection across the x-axis. f(−x) would be the reflection across the y-axis.

17. [Systems of Linear Inequalities & Linear Programming]
Answer: B) (5, 0)

Worked solution: Evaluate P at each vertex: (0, 0) → 0; (5, 0) → 15; (0, 4) → 8; (2, 3) → 6 + 6 = 12. The largest value is 15, at (5, 0).

18. [Probability from Two-Way Tables]
Answer: A) 0.15

Worked solution: “And” means the single cell where both are true: 30 students. Divide by the grand total, P = 30/200 = 0.15.

19. [Quadratic Functions & Equations]
Answer: 2

Worked solution: Step 1: a = 1 and b = −4. Step 2: x = −(−4) ÷ (2·1) = 4 ÷ 2 = 2.

20. [Absolute Value Equations & Inequalities]
Answer: D) No solution

Worked solution: An absolute value is a distance, so it can never equal a negative number. There is no value of x that makes |x − 4| equal −2, so the equation has no solution.

21. [Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences]
Answer: 160

Worked solution: a₁ = 5 and r = 2. aₙ = a₁ rⁿ⁻¹ gives a₆ = 5 · 2⁵ = 5 · 32 = 160. Check by listing: 5, 10, 20, 40, 80, 160 ✓.

22. [Inverse Functions]
Answer: C) f(x) = 2x + 5

Worked solution: f(x) = 2x + 5 is a straight line with a non-zero slope, so it passes both the vertical and the horizontal line test. x² and |x| fail the horizontal line test (two inputs share an output), and a constant function sends every input to the same output, so neither can have a function inverse.

23. [Function Transformations & Vertex Form]
Answer: A) horizontal compression by a factor of 1/2

Worked solution: Inside changes act on x and run opposite to what you see. Multiplying the input by 2 makes the graph reach each value twice as fast, so it compresses horizontally by a factor of 1/2.

24. [Systems of Linear Inequalities & Linear Programming]
Answer: 30

Worked solution: The vertices are (0, 0), (10, 0) and (0, 10). P = 0 at (0, 0), P = 20 at (10, 0) and P = 3(10) = 30 at (0, 10). The largest value is 30.
