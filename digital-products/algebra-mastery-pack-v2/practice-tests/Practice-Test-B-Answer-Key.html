Practice Test B — Answer Key & Worked Solutions

One worked solution per question. Mark the working as well as the final answer.

1. [Solving Linear Equations (for x)]
Answer: C) x = 3

Worked solution: Step 1: collect x-terms on one side and constants on the other → 5x − 2x = 6 + 3. Step 2: combine → 3x = 9. Step 3: divide by 3 → x = 3. Check: 5(3) − 3 = 12 and 2(3) + 6 = 12 ✓.

2. [Literal Equations & Solving for y]
Answer: A) r = C ÷ (2π)

Worked solution: Step 1: divide both sides by 2π. Step 2: r = C ÷ (2π).

3. [Linear Inequalities]
Answer: A) x < −4

Worked solution: Step 1: divide both sides by −2. Step 2: dividing by a negative flips the sign → x < −4.

4. [Systems of Linear Equations]
Answer: A) (5, 2)

Worked solution: Elimination: Step 1: add the equations → (x + x) + (y − y) = 7 + 3 → 2x = 10. Step 2: x = 5. Step 3: plug back → 5 + y = 7 → y = 2. Solution: (5, 2).

5. [Functions: Notation, Domain & Range]
Answer: B) 7

Worked solution: Step 1: substitute 3 for x → g(3) = 3² − 2. Step 2: square → 9 − 2. Step 3: subtract → 7.

6. [Linear Functions: Slope, Intercepts & Equations]
Answer: A) (0, 1)

Worked solution: The y-intercept is where x = 0: y = 2(0) + 1 = 1, so the line crosses the y-axis at (0, 1).

7. [Quadratic Functions & Equations]
Answer: 3

Worked solution: Step 1: factor → (x − 3)(x + 3) = 0. Step 2: x = 3 or x = −3. The positive solution is 3.

8. [Polynomials: Operations & Factoring]
Answer: A) x² + 5x + 6

Worked solution: FOIL: First x·x = x²; Outer x·3 = 3x; Inner 2·x = 2x; Last 2·3 = 6. Combine: x² + 5x + 6.

9. [Radical & Rational Expressions]
Answer: A) x = 12

Worked solution: Step 1: multiply both sides by 2 → x = 6 × 2. Step 2: x = 12.

10. [Exponential Functions & Growth/Decay]
Answer: A) exponential decay

Worked solution: Step 1: the base b = 0.8 is between 0 and 1. Step 2: 0 < b < 1 means decay.

11. [Data: Measures of Center & Spread]
Answer: 4

Worked solution: Step 1: count how often each value appears → 4 appears twice, all others once. Step 2: the mode is 4.

12. [Scatter Plots, Line of Best Fit & Correlation]
Answer: A) approximates the overall trend of the data

Worked solution: The line of best fit summarizes the general direction of the points; it does not need to touch every point.

13. [Absolute Value Equations & Inequalities]
Answer: B) x = 8 or x = −2

Worked solution: Split into two cases. Case 1: x − 3 = 5 → x = 8. Case 2: x − 3 = −5 → x = −2. Check: |8 − 3| = 5 ✓ and |−2 − 3| = |−5| = 5 ✓.

14. [Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences]
Answer: 79

Worked solution: a₁ = 3 and d = 4. aₙ = a₁ + (n − 1)d gives a₂₀ = 3 + 19(4) = 3 + 76 = 79.

15. [Inverse Functions]
Answer: 2

Worked solution: f⁻¹(0) asks which input gives an output of 0. Solve 3x − 6 = 0 → 3x = 6 → x = 2. Check: f(2) = 3(2) − 6 = 0 ✓.

16. [Function Transformations & Vertex Form]
Answer: A) shifted right 2 units

Worked solution: Changes inside the function act on the input and run opposite to the sign. f(x − 2) needs an input 2 larger to produce the same output, so the graph moves right 2 units.

17. [Systems of Linear Inequalities & Linear Programming]
Answer: D) (1, 1)

Worked solution: Check the sum first, then the signs. (1, 1): 1 + 1 = 2 ≤ 4 ✓, both coordinates non-negative ✓. (3, 2) gives 5, which is more than 4. (−1, 2) fails x ≥ 0. (4, 1) gives 5, which is more than 4.

18. [Probability from Two-Way Tables]
Answer: 0.25

Worked solution: The “given walks” shrinks the sample space to the 120 walkers. Of those, 30 are late, so P = 30/120 = 0.25.

19. [Quadratic Functions & Equations]
Answer: A) x = −4 or 2

Worked solution: Step 1: factor → (x + 4)(x − 2) = 0. Step 2: x + 4 = 0 → x = −4; x − 2 = 0 → x = 2.

20. [Polynomials: Operations & Factoring]
Answer: A) (x + 4)(x − 4)

Worked solution: Step 1: recognize a difference of squares a² − b² with a = x and b = 4. Step 2: x² − 16 = (x + 4)(x − 4).

21. [Radical & Rational Expressions]
Answer: 9

Worked solution: Step 1: subtract 1 from both sides → x/3 = 3. Step 2: multiply by 3 → x = 9.

22. [Exponential Functions & Growth/Decay]
Answer: A) exponential growth

Worked solution: Step 1: the base b = 1.5 is greater than 1. Step 2: b > 1 means growth.

23. [Data: Measures of Center & Spread]
Answer: 7

Worked solution: Step 1: find the max = 9 and the min = 2. Step 2: range = max − min = 9 − 2 = 7.

24. [Scatter Plots, Line of Best Fit & Correlation]
Answer: A) weak or no linear correlation

Worked solution: r ranges from −1 to 1; values near 0 mean there is little to no linear relationship.
