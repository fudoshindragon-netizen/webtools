Practice Test A — Answer Key & Worked Solutions

One worked solution per question. Mark the working as well as the final answer.

1. [Solving Linear Equations (for x)]
Answer: B) x = 4

Worked solution: Step 1: subtract 6 from both sides → 2x = 8. Step 2: divide both sides by 2 → x = 4. Check: 2(4) + 6 = 8 + 6 = 14 ✓.

2. [Literal Equations & Solving for y]
Answer: A) b = y − mx

Worked solution: Step 1: subtract mx from both sides. Step 2: b = y − mx.

3. [Linear Inequalities]
Answer: A) x < 4

Worked solution: Step 1: subtract 3 from both sides → x < 4. (No sign flip, because we subtracted, not multiplied/divided by a negative.)

4. [Systems of Linear Equations]
Answer: A) (3, 6)

Worked solution: Substitution: Step 1: replace y with 2x in the second equation → x + 2x = 9. Step 2: 3x = 9, so x = 3. Step 3: y = 2(3) = 6. Solution: (3, 6). Check: 6 = 2(3) ✓ and 3 + 6 = 9 ✓.

5. [Functions: Notation, Domain & Range]
Answer: C) 7

Worked solution: Step 1: substitute 2 for x → f(2) = 3(2) + 1. Step 2: multiply → 6 + 1. Step 3: add → 7.

6. [Linear Functions: Slope, Intercepts & Equations]
Answer: B) 2

Worked solution: In slope-intercept form y = mx + b, m is the slope (the coefficient of x). Here m = 2.

7. [Quadratic Functions & Equations]
Answer: A) x = −2 or −3

Worked solution: Step 1: factor → (x + 2)(x + 3) = 0. Step 2: set each factor to 0 → x + 2 = 0 or x + 3 = 0. Step 3: x = −2 or x = −3.

8. [Polynomials: Operations & Factoring]
Answer: A) 4x² + x + 5

Worked solution: Step 1: combine like terms → (3x² + x²) + (2x − x) + (1 + 4). Step 2: = 4x² + x + 5.

9. [Radical & Rational Expressions]
Answer: A) 5√2

Worked solution: Step 1: write 50 as 25 × 2. Step 2: √50 = √25 · √2 = 5√2.

10. [Exponential Functions & Growth/Decay]
Answer: A) 3

Worked solution: Step 1: substitute x = 0 → y = 3·2⁰. Step 2: 2⁰ = 1, so y = 3 × 1 = 3. The initial value a = 3.

11. [Data: Measures of Center & Spread]
Answer: 5

Worked solution: Step 1: add → 2 + 4 + 4 + 6 + 9 = 25. Step 2: divide by the count 5 → 25 ÷ 5 = 5.

12. [Scatter Plots, Line of Best Fit & Correlation]
Answer: A) positive

Worked solution: When both variables move in the same direction (up together), the correlation is positive.

13. [Absolute Value Equations & Inequalities]
Answer: C) x = 7 or x = −7

Worked solution: |x| = 7 means x is 7 units from 0 on the number line, so x = 7 or x = −7. Check: |7| = 7 ✓ and |−7| = 7 ✓.

14. [Arithmetic & Geometric Sequences]
Answer: C) 16

Worked solution: The common difference is d = 7 − 4 = 3, and 10 − 7 = 3 and 13 − 10 = 3 confirm it. The next term is 13 + 3 = 16.

15. [Inverse Functions]
Answer: A) 2

Worked solution: The inverse undoes f. Since f sends 2 to 9, f⁻¹ sends 9 back to 2, so f⁻¹(9) = 2.

16. [Function Transformations & Vertex Form]
Answer: A) shifted up 3 units

Worked solution: The +3 is outside the function, so it changes the output: every y-value is 3 larger. That moves the whole graph up 3 units.

17. [Systems of Linear Inequalities & Linear Programming]
Answer: D) (−1, 0)

Worked solution: Test each point in both inequalities. (−1, 0): 0 > −1 − 1 = −2 is true, but 0 ≤ 2(−1) = −2 is false, so it fails. The others pass both: (1, 2): 2 > 0 ✓ and 2 ≤ 2 ✓; (0, 0): 0 > −1 ✓ and 0 ≤ 0 ✓; (2, 3): 3 > 1 ✓ and 3 ≤ 4 ✓.

18. [Probability from Two-Way Tables]
Answer: B) 0.40

Worked solution: 80 of the 200 students ride the bus, so P = 80/200 = 0.40.

19. [Quadratic Functions & Equations]
Answer: A) x = 5 or −1

Worked solution: Step 1: factor → (x − 5)(x + 1) = 0. Step 2: x − 5 = 0 → x = 5; x + 1 = 0 → x = −1.

20. [Polynomials: Operations & Factoring]
Answer: A) 6x²

Worked solution: Step 1: multiply the coefficients → 2 × 3 = 6. Step 2: multiply the variables → x · x = x². Result: 6x².

21. [Radical & Rational Expressions]
Answer: 25

Worked solution: Step 1: square both sides → (√x)² = 5². Step 2: x = 25. Check: √25 = 5 ✓.

22. [Exponential Functions & Growth/Decay]
Answer: 8

Worked solution: Step 1: substitute 3 for x → y = 2³. Step 2: 2³ = 8.

23. [Data: Measures of Center & Spread]
Answer: 4

Worked solution: Step 1: the values are already ordered → 2, 4, 4, 6, 9. Step 2: the middle value (3rd of 5) is 4.

24. [Scatter Plots, Line of Best Fit & Correlation]
Answer: A) negative

Worked solution: When one variable rises while the other falls, the correlation is negative.
