## Open it

Double-click **Algebra-Mastery-Pack.html**. It opens in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari — any of them). Nothing to install, no account, no internet connection needed.

If you prefer the folder edition, open `folder-edition/index.html` instead — but that one has to be served from a web address rather than opened from a file, so the single-file version above is the one to use on a laptop or phone.

## Use it

1. Pick a topic from the grid.
2. Read the step-by-step method, the rules or table, and the worked example.
3. Answer the practice questions. Multiple choice: pick one and press **Check answer**. Short answer: type the number only — for `x = 8` type `8`, for `y = -3.5` type `-3.5` — then press **Check answer**.
4. The worked solution appears under the question — every step, not just the final answer.
5. Your progress and mastery percentage are saved in this browser automatically. Closing the tab does not lose it.

## How to use it well

- Attempt the question before you check it. Reading the solution first feels productive and is not.
- If you get several wrong in one topic, do not redo them immediately. Re-read the method, then wait a day and redo the set from a blank page. Same-day repetition tests your short-term memory; next-day repetition tests whether you learned it.
- Write the steps down on paper as you go. This pack gives you the model answer; the exam gives you a blank page.
- Take Test A a week after you finish the topics, then B a week later, then C. If a topic keeps costing you marks, go back to that topic's method rather than doing more questions.
