Accepts vertex form (y = 2(x − 3)² + 4), standard form (y = 2x² − 12x + 22), and factored form (y = 2(x − 1)(x − 5)). Use ^ for exponents.

Parent function: y = x²

  • Domain: (−∞, ∞) — every real x can be squared.
  • Range: [0, ∞) — squaring never gives a negative.
  • Vertex: (0, 0) — 0² = 0 is the minimum.
  • Axis of symmetry: x = 0.
  • Decreasing: (−∞, 0); Increasing: (0, ∞).

Why it's symmetric: because (−x)² = x², a point any distance left of the y-axis sits at the same height as the matching point the same distance right of it — that's the mirror symmetry.
