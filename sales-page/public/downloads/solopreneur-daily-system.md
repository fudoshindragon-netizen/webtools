# The Solopreneur Daily System

A one-page framework for solo founders to plan, execute, and recharge.

---

## Morning Block (60 min) — Plan & Recharge
- 5 min: **Gratitude** — 3 things you're grateful for
- 10 min: **Review priorities** — What matters most today?
- 15 min: **Deep work** — Write/plan the biggest task
- 30 min: **High-impact task #1** (the ONE thing)

## Deep Work Block (120 min) — Create
- One focused session with **no notifications**
- Work on your hardest task first
- Single-tab mode: close Slack, email, social media

## Midday Reset (30 min)
- Walk, stretch, or exercise
- Eat away from your desk
- No screens

## Afternoon Block (90 min) — Execute
- **Task batching**: Group similar tasks together
  - Emails & messages: 20 min
  - Content/shipping: 40 min
  - Admin & planning: 30 min

## Evening Review (15 min)
- What got done? (celebrate wins)
- What's blocked? (note for tomorrow)
- Set tomorrow's **ONE priority**

---

## Weekly Rituals

| Day       | Focus Area          | Suggested Task                         |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Monday    | Plan & Audit        | Review metrics, set weekly goals       |
| Tuesday   | Create & Build      | Deep work on product/content           |
| Wednesday | Connect & Market    | Outreach, social media, partnerships   |
| Thursday  | Optimize            | Fix bottlenecks, refine systems        |
| Friday    | Ship & Review       | Launch, publish, week review           |
| Weekend   | Rest & Explore      | No work — read, reflect, recharge      |

---

## Rules of Thumb

- **Parkinson's Law**: Work expands to fill the time — set shorter deadlines
- **The 80/20 Rule**: 20% of tasks produce 80% of results — find and do those first
- **Two-Minute Rule**: If it takes <2 minutes, do it now
- **Weekly Audit**: If a task earned zero revenue or zero learning in the past week, drop it
