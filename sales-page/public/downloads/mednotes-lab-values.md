# MedNotes Quick Reference: Essential Lab Values

## Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- **WBC**: 4.5–11.0 × 10³/µL
- **RBC**: Male 4.7–6.1, Female 4.2–5.4 × 10⁶/µL
- **Hemoglobin**: Male 13.5–17.5, Female 12.0–16.0 g/dL
- **Hematocrit**: Male 41–53%, Female 36–46%
- **Platelets**: 150–450 × 10³/µL

## Basic Metabolic Panel (BMP)
- **Sodium (Na+)**: 136–145 mEq/L
- **Potassium (K+)**: 3.5–5.0 mEq/L
- **Chloride (Cl-)**: 98–106 mEq/L
- **CO2 (Bicarbonate)**: 23–30 mEq/L
- **BUN**: 7–20 mg/dL
- **Creatinine**: 0.6–1.2 mg/dL
- **Glucose**: 70–100 mg/dL (fasting)
- **Calcium**: 8.5–10.2 mg/dL

## Mnemonic: BUN:Cr Ratio
Normal ratio is 10:1 to 20:1.
- >20:1 = Prerenal (dehydration, CHF)
- <10:1 = Intrarenal (ATN, interstitial nephritis)

## Arterial Blood Gas (ABG) Quick Guide
- **pH**: 7.35–7.45
- **PaCO2**: 35–45 mmHg
- **PaO2**: 80–100 mmHg
- **HCO3-**: 22–26 mEq/L
- **O2 Sat**: 95–100%

### ABG Interpretation (ROME)
- **Respiratory Opposite** — pH up = PaCO2 down (alkalosis)
- **Metabolic Equal** — pH up = HCO3 up (alkalosis)

## Cardiac Markers
- **Troponin I**: <0.04 ng/mL (elevated = MI)
- **CK-MB**: 3–5% of total CK
- **BNP**: <100 pg/mL (elevated in HF)
