What's inside

Learn it, look it up, test yourself. The book is organized in five parts:

  • Part I — Study Guide. Fourteen short chapters: binary and number systems; Ohm's law, power and energy; reactance and impedance; series and parallel circuits; transformers and power factor; three-phase delta and wye; motors (service factor, inrush, shunt speed); AC drives and dynamic braking; fuses and current transformers; switches and grounding; logic gates; PLC contacts, data types and sinking/sourcing; analog inputs and scaling; and a motor start/stop ladder rung.
  • Part II — Formula & Reference Sheets. Thirteen one-topic sheets that put every key relationship on a page.
  • Parts III–V — Test yourself. 77 practice questions (multiple choice and calculation), a complete answer key with a one-line explanation of every answer, and a 100-card flashcard drill for quick recall.

The paperback is 42 pages, 6×9 inches, ISBN 9798141679100. The Kindle edition is the same content.

A study aid only. It does not teach how to perform electrical work safely and does not reproduce the National Electrical Code, NFPA 70E or OSHA rules. It is not a substitute for your course, employer training or the governing standards. No claims that it will help you pass an exam, and it is not certified or approved training.

It is also a different book from the Electrical Safety Quick Reference Guide ($15 on Gumroad), which covers OSHA, NEC and NFPA safety planning. This title is the controls-and-power study guide: formulas, worked examples, practice questions and flashcards.
