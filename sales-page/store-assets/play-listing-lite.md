## Full description

Luna Cycle is a private period calendar for your phone.

Log the day a period starts and the day it ends. For any day, you can record flow, symptoms, mood, and a note. The calendar marks the days you logged and shows an estimate of the next period from those dates. It also shades an estimated fertile window: nine days ending 14 days before the next predicted period. That shading is a calendar estimate, not a test. Days outside the blue mark are not safe days.

You can set a reminder for a time you choose. The choices are a daily pill, a weekly patch day, ring in and out dates, or a shot date. The reminder is a notification on this phone. You can decline notifications and keep using the log.

What you enter stays on the phone. There is no account. The app does not send your log anywhere. You can export a JSON file, import a file you already have, or delete all of it.

Luna Cycle is not medical advice.
