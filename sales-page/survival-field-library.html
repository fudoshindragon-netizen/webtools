What is in the folders

  • Survival fundamentals — the 1999 multiservice pocket manual Survival, Evasion, and Recovery (FM 21-76-1), plus the USMC Mountain Warfare Training Center summer and winter survival course handbooks.
  • First aid and medicine — FM 4-25.11 First Aid (2002), the 2004 Borden Institute Emergency War Surgery handbook (for physicians, not household first aid), and the 2005 HHS Pandemic Influenza Plan (historical; not current public-health guidance).
  • Shelter and construction — Beard’s 1914 Shelters, Shacks, and Shanties, plus Army manuals on carpentry, concrete and masonry, and battlefield survivability positions.
  • Cold weather, mountains and movement — FM 31-70 Basic Cold Weather Manual, FM 3-97.6 Mountain Operations, and TC 21-24 Rappelling.
  • Land navigation — FM 3-25.26 Map Reading and Land Navigation, and a 42-page USMC Combat Skills land-navigation extract.
  • Historic campcraft and food — Beard’s 1920 Book of Camp-Lore and Woodcraft, Gibson’s Camp Life in the Woods, Harding’s 1907 Deadfalls and Snares, and Rorer’s 1912 Canning and Preserving (historical only — do not use it as modern canning safety guidance).
  • Combatives reference — FM 3-25.150 Combatives (2002). A historical training reference, not a course.

Honest limits

This is an educational reference, not medical, legal, tactical or professional advice, and not a substitute for certified training or current official guidance.

Some of these manuals are decades old. Older manuals, especially medical ones, can contain outdated guidance. Check current CDC and American Red Cross first-aid guidance before you rely on a technique. For home canning, follow current USDA / National Center for Home Food Preservation guidance. For public-health questions, check current CDC advice rather than the 2005 HHS plan.

Three candidate files were reviewed and left out: a 2002 Army survival manual whose copy is marked distribution-restricted, a 1987 Nuclear War Survival Skills file that carries copyrighted additions, and a USDA canning-guide copy that contained third-party editorial notes in the process tables. The short, unlimited-distribution manual FM 21-76-1 is included instead of the restricted survival manual.
